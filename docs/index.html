Minicourse

A Practical Guide to Multimodal Learning
for Disease Classification & Survival Prediction

Whole-slide pathology + mpMRI + clinical records, fused three different ways,
to predict prostate cancer biochemical recurrence.

This course walks through an entire multimodal pipeline end to end: preparing three very different data modalities, building encoders for each, comparing three ways of fusing them, training with a censoring-aware survival objective, and evaluating with the visualizations that make a model's predictions inspectable rather than opaque. Every section pairs a written explanation with the real, runnable code behind it, and links to a companion notebook you can open directly in Colab.

Course Contents

  • 01BackgroundWhat each modality sees, why combine them, classification vs. survival framing
  • 02Data PreparationTissue detection, patch extraction, mpMRI & clinical preprocessing, label/split prep
  • 03Model ArchitecturePer-modality encoders, ABMIL pooling, and three fusion strategies side by side
  • 04Training ProtocolsSlide-level training rows, discrete-time survival loss, cross-validation
  • 05InferenceRunning trained models and aggregating slide-level output back to the patient level
  • 06Post-processingDecision thresholds and risk-group stratification
  • 07Evaluation & VisualizationAUROC/C-index comparisons, Kaplan-Meier curves, attention heatmaps
  • 08ConclusionTakeaways, limitations, and where to go next
  • 09QuizCheck your understanding
